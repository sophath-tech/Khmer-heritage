Khmer Heritage

Preserving Khmer Heritage, Literature, Culture and Civilization.

About This Project

Khmer Heritage is a digital knowledge project dedicated to researching, documenting and preserving Khmer cultural heritage.

Main Topics

- Khmer Literature
- Khmer Language
- Khmer Civilization
- Khmer Culture and Traditions
- Historical Research
- Khmer Intellectual Heritage

Mission

To preserve, study and share the richness of Khmer heritage for present and future generations.
